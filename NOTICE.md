# Notice

The embedding, cosine-recall, and reranker score-fusion approach is adapted
from `sourwater487/Ombre-Brain`, itself based on `P0luz/Ombre-Brain`.
Both projects are distributed under the MIT License.

The implementation remains independent and intentionally excludes Ombre's
personal-assistant, diary, task, persona, reminder, and gateway features.
Only plot-retrieval metadata such as emotion coordinates, importance, tags,
cooldown, and limited relationship expansion are retained.

The visible-window exclusion, native `/hide` synchronization, separate
history/recall injection, and graceful recall degradation were also reviewed
against the public ST-BaiBai-Book project. No ST-BaiBai-Book source code or
assets are included in this distribution.
